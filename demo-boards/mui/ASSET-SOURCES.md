## Seeded prompt metadata

The seven entries in the Velloo asset store are representative **demo prompts** added at the user's request to demonstrate the Image panel's prompt experience. The underlying photos remain the sourced images listed above, and the map remains an authored SVG. They were not generated by the prompts. The metadata's `generatedAt` value is the seeding time; it is not a provenance claim about when the image was created. No successful paid generation result was used.
